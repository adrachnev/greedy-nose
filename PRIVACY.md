# Greedy Nose — Privacy Policy

_Effective: 2026-09-16_

Greedy Nose is a personal, early-stage project built by an individual developer — not a
company — to notify you when a payee you have marked "Bad" charges your bank account. This
page explains what data the app touches and what happens to it.

## What data we access

With your explicit consent, Greedy Nose reads your bank account's transaction history — dates,
amounts, payee/merchant names, and (where your bank provides it) IBANs — through
[Enable Banking](https://enablebanking.com), a PSD2-licensed Account Information Service
Provider (AISP). We only ever **read** this data. Greedy Nose cannot move money, make payments,
or change anything in your bank account.

## Why we access it

So the app can tell you the moment a payee you have flagged as untrusted ("Bad") charges you,
and optionally warn you if a trusted ("Good") payee charges more than a limit you set. That is
the entire purpose of the app.

## Where your data lives

- Your consent to read your account is tied to your own connected backend instance, not to a
  shared server we control.
- Which payees you have marked Good or Bad, and any per-payee limits, are set by you in the app
  and currently live on your own device/backend.
- We run no analytics, ads, or trackers, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone
  except Enable Banking, which needs it to reach your bank on your behalf under its own PSD2
  license and its own privacy policy.

## Retention

Transaction data is fetched to show you your own charges; it is not retained by us beyond what
running the app requires. You can revoke Greedy Nose's access to your account at any time,
either through your bank's own online banking/app or by disconnecting inside Greedy Nose.

## Your rights

Since this involves EU/EEA bank data, you have the usual rights over data derived from your
account: to ask what is held, to have it deleted, and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact
us for any of these — see below.

## Changes

This app is under active development, and this policy may change as new features (such as push
notifications or server-side storage) are added. Material changes will be reflected here with an
updated effective date.

## What the app does

Greedy Nose reads your bank transaction history (via [Enable Banking](https://enablebanking.com),
a licensed account information intermediary) and notifies you when a payee you have marked "Bad"
charges your account, or when a "Good" payee exceeds a limit you set. It is purely informational —
it does not initiate payments, manage budgets, or give financial advice.

## Your bank connection

Using Greedy Nose requires granting it read-only access to your bank account via Enable Banking,
limited to banks Enable Banking supports in the EEA. You can revoke this access at any time,
